Who am I?

I am Mehedi, an undergraduate Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) student driven by curiosity about how physical laws translate into smart, automated technologies. My academic journey focuses on circuit design, microcontrollers, and sustainable energy systems.

Beyond academics and circuit boards, I am an avid traveler. Exploring remote landscapes, historical sites, and diverse cultures refines my perspective, boosts my creativity, and reminds me of the beauty of our planet.
